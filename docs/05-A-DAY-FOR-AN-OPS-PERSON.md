# 05 · A day for an Ops person

How the planned Ops system helps an ops owner through a normal working day. It is
built from the Ops flow ([04](04-OPS-FLOW.md)) and the problems in today's way of
working (Excel, WhatsApp and Outlook, with chasing done from memory). The details
of each step are agreed feature by feature in Phase 0, step 3.

## Today, without the system

- Client agreements sit in Outlook; nobody is reminded when a client hasn't signed.
- CV sets go to clients by hand; nobody counts the 7 days the client has to claim a CV.
- Client feedback is chased only when someone remembers.
- Interview times are agreed over many emails and WhatsApp messages.
- Asma learns about placements late (once, a January joining on 30 March), so invoices go out late.
- Guarantee dates are worked out by hand, if at all.

## The same day, with the Ops system

### 9:00 · Open the Ops inbox

The first screen is the **Ops inbox** (it replaces the notebook, the sheets and the WhatsApp scroll): everything that needs this person today, in
one list, most urgent first.

- **Waiting for my approval:** messages the system has already drafted. For
  example: an ESA ready for a new client, a chase to a client who hasn't replied
  for 1 day, a CV set the TA marked ready, an invoice for a candidate who joins today.
- **Due today:** an intro call at 11:00, a countersign reminder for the Director,
  a guarantee ending this week.
- **Late:** anything past its time, for example an ESA not sent 2 hours after the
  intro call.

**How it helps:** nothing depends on memory. The system watches every timer and
puts the work in front of the right person. The flow behind each item is in
[04](04-OPS-FLOW.md).

### 9:30 · Approve and send

The ops owner opens each draft, edits it if needed, and presses **Approve**. The
system sends it from the Ops mailbox (or as an approved WhatsApp template), files a
copy, and starts the next timer. For example, after a CV set is sent the system
starts the 7-working-day ownership countdown.

**How it helps:** one click instead of writing every email; every message is logged
against the client.

### 10:00 · A new lead arrives

A website enquiry appears under **Leads**. The system has already checked whether
the company is a known client, and flagged it if it is banking or finance. The ops
owner books the intro call; the TA lead is invited automatically.

### 11:00 · Intro call, then the ESA

After the call, the ops owner saves the package, role and brief. The system drafts
the ESA from the template with the client's details. Any blank field blocks
sending. The ops owner approves it and it goes out within the 1-2 hours. If the
client does not sign, the system prepares the chase messages one by one, each for
approval, and finally creates a call task for Rushda.

### 13:00 · Client replies come in

The system reads the client's replies and suggests what each one means
("shortlisted: candidates A and B; rejected: candidate C, salary too high"). The ops owner
confirms, and the decision goes to the TA System straight away, so the TA sees it
on the opening without a phone call. If the client now has 5 shortlisted, the TA is
told to hold and monitor; if 3 or fewer are pending, the TA gets an add-on alert.

### 14:00 · Book an interview

The client gives times. The ops owner picks one; the system creates the Teams
meeting, sends the invite to the client's interviewers, and records the interview
in the TA System, where the TA confirms the candidate. The day before, the
reminders go out automatically.

### 15:00 · A placement

A TA presses **Place** in the TA System with the package, joining date and offer
letter. The Ops system gets the event at once: **Asma and the ops owner are told the
same day**, the joining date goes on the calendar, and on joining day the invoice
draft is waiting in the inbox.

### 16:30 · Money and guarantee

- The **Invoices** screen shows what is due, paid and late; late ones already have a
  chase drafted, with the 5% added.
- The **Guarantees** screen shows every placement's 90-day window. If a candidate
  leaves, the ops owner marks it; the system reopens the same opening in TA for a
  replacement and starts the 60-working-day clock.

### 17:30 · End of day

The inbox is empty or every item has a date. Nothing is waiting in someone's head.
On Mondays the weekly active-positions report for Mr Azeem is built automatically,
for the TA lead to check and send.

## What the Ops person never does again

- Copy client or candidate details from one place to another.
- Count days by hand (ESA, ownership, feedback, invoice, guarantee).
- Ask a TA on WhatsApp what happened to a candidate.
- Keep a separate sheet for clients, interviews or placements.
